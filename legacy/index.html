Scam awareness

Scam awareness

Simple habits that make common scams harder to pull off.

Pause when a message creates urgency

Contact the organization through its official app or a number you find independently.

Never share sign-in codes

Banks and legitimate services do not need you to send a PIN, password, or one-time code in a message.

Check links before opening them

Read the full domain carefully and avoid shortened links in unexpected messages.

Pause unusual payment requests

Gift cards, crypto, and urgent transfer requests are hard to reverse. Verify first.
